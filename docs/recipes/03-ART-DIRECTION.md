# Recipe 03: Art Direction and Assets

## Required inputs

Cultural or product anchors, approved references, chapter plan, accessibility needs, and budgets.

## Required outputs

Exact design tokens, anti-patterns, asset inventory, responsive variants, loading priority, and provenance.

## Stop conditions

Stop if a reference risks copying, source rights are unknown, or mobile composition is undefined.

## Context updates

Update `DESIGN_CONTEXT.md`, `ASSET_PLAN.md`, and relevant assumptions.

## Acceptance checks

Desktop and mobile each have intentional compositions; every asset has a job, owner, budget, and source record.
