# Recipe 01: Discovery

## Required inputs

User goal, current implementation, audience evidence, stable interfaces, and unresolved facts.

## Required outputs

Verified facts, assumptions, audience, primary action, first-viewport promise, and explicit boundaries.

## Stop conditions

Stop if identity, content authority, rights, or compatibility requirements are materially unclear.

## Context updates

Update `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` and `CONTENT_ASSUMPTIONS.md`.

## Acceptance checks

Facts and assumptions are separate; every claim has an owner or replacement condition.
