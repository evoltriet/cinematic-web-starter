# Design Context

## Art direction

Precision field equipment observed in changing weather, with cinematic atmosphere supporting technical clarity.

## Design tokens

Graphite, slate, optical blue, safety amber, warm gray; machined edges and measured spacing.

## Responsive composition

Mobile prioritizes silhouette, one mechanism, and clear comparison; desktop expands the material sequence.

## Motion language

Controlled reveal and one local mechanism story; no product orbit or global pointer response.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid neon gaming language, floating specification cards, impossible reflections, and unverified superlatives.
