# Design Context

## Art direction

Nocturnal museum architecture with reverent framing and tactile archival surfaces.

## Design tokens

Charcoal, verdigris, parchment, ember; sharp frames and narrow pools of light.

## Responsive composition

Mobile shows one artifact and its interpretation at a time; desktop may use paired vitrines.

## Motion language

Slow reveal and one finite light passage; artifacts themselves remain visually stable.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid treasure-hunt gamification, faux antiquity, floating cards, and dramatic object spins.
